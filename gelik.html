How to play — the turn loop & what actions mean
  1. Round 1: Move action → start Inspire Courage (+2 attack, +2 damage, +2 vs fear/charm to all allies who hear you). Then Standard action → cast a spell like Haste. You can still take a 5-ft step.
  2. Every round after: Inspire Courage keeps going for Free (tap a Bardic Performance dot). Spend your turn to cast, shoot the shortbow, or move.
  3. When in doubt: keep the song up and plink with the shortbow. Only one performance at a time.
Standard
Your main thing: cast a spell, make one attack, or normally start a performance.
Move
Move your speed, draw a weapon — or (at level 10) start a performance.
Full-Round
Your whole turn: a Full Attack (all your attacks) or a long spell. You still get a 5-ft step.
Swift
A tiny bonus action, one per round (some spells & items).
Free
Talk, drop something — and maintain a performance.
5-ft step
Shuffle 5 ft, no risk. Allowed even after a full-round action if you didn't otherwise move.

Spellbook — type your spells, tap slots to cast

Ask your DM which spells you know, then type them in. You're a spontaneous caster: cast any spell you know as long as a slot of that level is open. Tap a slot to spend it; use + / − to match your real slot count.

Playing Gelik

  • He's a writer, not a singer. Perform by talking — snarky pep talks, heckling villains, dramatic storytelling.
  • Say the line out loud in character when you start a performance. That turns a dice bonus into Gelik.
  • Lean into the snark. Sharply dressed, ink-stained, mean-witted little showoff.
  • Out of combat you're the face & the brains. With Bardic Knowledge you can attempt any Knowledge check.
  • Stay safe. Keep within ~30 ft of allies so they hear you, but behind the front line.
